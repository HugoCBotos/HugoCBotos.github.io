Theorem 7.1: (Dominated convergence theorem for series)

Consider a double sequence pnkp_{nk}. Assume that ak=limn→∞pnka_k=\lim_{n \to \infty}p_{nk} exists for all kk.

Define pnk=cnkzk,ak=(−1)k−1zkk,bk=|z|kk.p_{nk}=c_{nk}z^k,\qquad a_k=\frac{(-1)^{k-1}z^k}{k},\qquad b_k=\frac{|z|^k}{k}. For each fixed kk, pnk→akp_{nk}\to a_k, and |pnk|≤bk|p_{nk}|\leq b_k. Since b1+b2+b3+⋯=|z|+|z|22+|z|33+⋯<∞b_1+b_2+b_3+\cdots=|z|+\frac{|z|^2}{2}+\frac{|z|^3}{3}+\cdots<\infty for |z|<1|z|<1, Theorem 7.1 gives log⁡(1+z)=z−z22+z33−⋯.\log(1+z)=z-\frac{z^2}{2}+\frac{z^3}{3}-\cdots.

QED
