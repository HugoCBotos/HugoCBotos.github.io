📖 BNN Overview

The big picture: what BNNs are, why they matter, and how the different approximation methods compare. Start here.

overview

1. Neural Networks

What is a neural network? Linear layers, activation functions, forward pass, backpropagation. The bare minimum.

beginner

2. Bayesian Inference

Prior, likelihood, posterior, Bayes' theorem. The core philosophy of treating unknowns as distributions.

beginner

3. Probability Distributions

Random variables, PDFs, the Gaussian distribution, expectation, variance — the minimal probability toolkit.

beginner

4. Types of Uncertainty

Aleatoric vs epistemic uncertainty. Why we care. What BNNs give you that normal NNs don't.

intermediate

5. KL Divergence

A measure of "distance" between probability distributions. The workhorse of variational Bayesian methods.

intermediate

6. The ELBO

The Evidence Lower BOund — the objective function we actually optimize. Connects Bayesian inference to optimization.

intermediate

7. Variational Inference

The most popular approach: learn a distribution over weights by backpropagation. Full PyTorch code example.

advanced

8. MC Dropout

The simplest trick: use regular dropout at test time to get uncertainty. Near-zero extra cost.

intermediate

9. Laplace Approximation

Fit a Gaussian around the optimal weights after training. Elegant mathematics, simple implementation.

advanced

10. MCMC & HMC

The gold standard — Hamiltonian Monte Carlo explained conceptually. Too expensive in practice but sets the benchmark.

advanced

📖 Paper Index

Organized catalog of all BNN reference papers. Use this to navigate the research literature.

reference

Bayes by Backprop

Blundell et al., 2015 — Learn weight distributions via the reparameterization trick. The canonical VI method for BNNs.

variational

Probabilistic Backprop

Hernández-Lobato & Adams, 2015 — Moment-matching propagation for fast, deterministic Bayesian inference.

variational

Normalizing Flows

Louizos & Welling, 2017 — Structured posteriors with normalizing flows for richer uncertainty.

flows

Dropout as Bayesian

Gal & Ghahramani, 2016 — Dropout as approximate variational inference. The theoretical foundation for MC Dropout.

dropout

Laplace Redux

Daxberger et al., 2021 — Modern Laplace approximation with Kronecker-factored Hessians. Scalable and competitive.

laplace

BNN Posteriors

Izmailov et al., 2021 — What are BNN posteriors really like? An HMC-based empirical study revealing complex correlations.

hmc

HMC Intro

Betancourt, 2017 — A conceptual introduction to Hamiltonian Monte Carlo. Essential intuition-building.

tutorial

Hands-on BNN

Jospin et al., 2022 — Tutorial-style review with practical PyTorch code examples for VI, MC Dropout, Laplace.
