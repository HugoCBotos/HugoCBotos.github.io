Bayesian Neural Networks A beginner-friendly guide for mathematicians

The short story: A normal neural network gives you a single guess. A Bayesian neural network gives you a guess plus a measure of how uncertain you should be. That extra uncertainty information is crucial when you're making decisions based on the network's output — for example, in medical diagnosis, self-driving cars, or any scientific application.

🔁 The Big Picture in One Paragraph

A neural network is just a function f(x; w) where x is the input and w are the weights. Normal training finds a single best w.

In a Bayesian neural network, we replace the fixed weights w with a probability distribution over weights. Instead of saying "the weight is 0.5", we say "the weight is probably around 0.5, but could be a bit more or less".

The catch: the exact distribution is impossible to compute. So we approximate it. The different approaches (VI, MC Dropout, Laplace, HMC) are different approximation strategies. Each trades off computational cost against approximation quality.

⚡ Quick Comparison of Methods

Method Idea Bayesian part happens... Cost vs Normal NN Quality
Bayes by Backprop Learn mean & variance of each weight During training (learns μ, σ) ≈ 2× slower, 2× parameters Good, but assumes Gaussian
MC Dropout Keep dropout on at test time, run multiple times Both (dropout regularizes training, MC averaging at test time) ≈ 1× training (may need more epochs), T× test cost Surprisingly good
Laplace Gaussian around the optimal weights (post-hoc) After training (purely post-hoc) ≈ 1× training + Hessian computation Good for last layer
HMC Sample from the exact posterior (no approximation) After training (but needs a completely different training procedure) 100-1000× — usually impractical Gold standard (exact)
💡 Recommendation: If you're just starting out, read pages 1–6 to build the foundations, then jump to MC Dropout (page 8) — it's the simplest thing that actually works. Come back to the other methods once you need more sophisticated uncertainty estimates.
