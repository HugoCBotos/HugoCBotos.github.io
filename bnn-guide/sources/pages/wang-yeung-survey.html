A Survey on Bayesian Deep Learning

Wang & Yeung — ACM Computing Surveys, 2020

📄 Paper: arXiv:1604.01662
📁 Source: 2020_Wang_Yeung_Bayesian_Deep_Learning_Survey.pdf
🎯 Core Idea

A comprehensive survey covering the full landscape of Bayesian deep learning: Bayesian neural networks, Bayesian convolutional networks, Bayesian recurrent networks, and deep Gaussian processes. Provides a unified taxonomy and notation.

Coverage Highlights

  • Bayesian Neural Networks: VI, MC Dropout, Laplace, MCMC, expectation propagation.
  • Bayesian CNNs: Convolutional variants, Bayesian pooling, spatial dropout.
  • Bayesian RNNs: Bayesian LSTMs, GRUs, with applications to time series and NLP.
  • Deep Gaussian Processes: Connections between BNNs and GPs, infinite-width limits (Neal, 1996).
  • Applications: Computer vision, natural language processing, reinforcement learning, medical imaging.

Taxonomy of Approaches

The paper organizes Bayesian deep learning methods along two axes:

  • Inference method: Variational, sampling-based (MCMC), or deterministic (Laplace, EP).
  • Model component: Full network, per-layer, or per-neuron uncertainty.

Best Used As

A reference for understanding the broad landscape. If you need to know which Bayesian method has been applied to which architecture (CNN, RNN, etc.), this is the place to look. However, note that it predates many important developments (Laplace Redux, Izmailov et al., normalizing flows).
