Bayesian Neural Networks: An Introduction and Survey

Goan & Fookes — Springer (Case Studies in Applied Bayesian Data Science), 2020

📄 Paper: arXiv:2006.12024
📁 Source: 2020_Goan_Fookes_Bayesian_Neural_Networks_Survey.pdf
🎯 Core Idea

A pedagogical survey that builds from first principles (probability theory, Bayesian inference) all the way to modern scalable VI for BNNs. Ideal for someone who wants to understand both the "why" and the "how" of BNNs.

Coverage

  • Foundations: Bayesian inference, MAP estimation, Laplace approximation, MCMC basics.
  • Variational inference: Mean-field VI, reparameterization trick, Bayes by Backprop, local reparameterization.
  • Scalable methods: Stochastic VI, mini-batch ELBO, variance reduction techniques.
  • Applications: Regression, classification, uncertainty quantification, active learning, reinforcement learning.
  • Software: Overview of Pyro, TensorFlow Probability, Edward, PyMC3.

Strengths

  • Very clear exposition of the mathematical foundations — good for newcomers.
  • Includes practical considerations: prior selection, hyperparameter tuning, computational budget.
  • Comparison of different approaches with consistent notation (rare in the field).

Best Used As

A first read if you're comfortable with basic probability and neural networks but new to Bayesian methods. After reading this, move to the more recent Magris et al. (2023) survey for the current state of the art.
