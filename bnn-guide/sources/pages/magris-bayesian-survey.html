Bayesian Learning for Neural Networks: A Survey

Magris & Iosifidis — arXiv:2304.02868, 2023

📄 Paper: arXiv:2304.02868
📁 Source: 2023_Magris_Iosifidis_Bayesian_Learning_NNs_Survey.pdf
🎯 Core Idea

The most recent and up-to-date survey (2023) on Bayesian neural networks. Covers modern developments including last-layer methods, scalable VI, connections to uncertainty quantification, out-of-distribution detection, and calibration. Essential for understanding the current state of the field.

Key Topics Covered

  • Modern VI: Factors, normalizing flows, implicit distributions, variational ensembles.
  • Last-layer methods: Only inferring uncertainty in the final layer (extremely scalable). Laplace (Daxberger et al.), last-layer VI, last-layer ensembles.
  • Deep Ensembles: Why they work so well (Lakshminarayanan et al., 2017) and their Bayesian interpretation.
  • Conformal prediction: Distribution-free uncertainty sets that can be combined with BNNs.
  • Out-of-distribution detection: Using BNN uncertainty to detect novel inputs.
  • Calibration: Expected Calibration Error (ECE), reliability diagrams, temperature scaling.
  • Benchmarking: Standardized evaluation protocols for BNN uncertainty (UCI, CIFAR-10/100, ImageNet).

Open Problems (as of 2023)

  • Scalable HMC: Efficient MCMC for large models remains open.
  • Proper scoring rules for uncertainty: Which metric to use? Log-likelihood, Brier score, CRPS, sharpness?
  • Benchmarking gap: Lack of standardized, real-world benchmarks for uncertainty.
  • Uncertainty in decision making: How to act on uncertainty (Bayesian decision theory) in deep learning pipelines.
  • Computational budget: The best method depends heavily on available compute — no one-size-fits-all solution.
💡 Why this is the best starting point: This survey (2023) subsumes most of the older surveys. If you read only one survey, make it this one. It provides a complete, up-to-date taxonomy and discusses practical considerations (compute, scalability, calibration) that matter for real-world deployment.
